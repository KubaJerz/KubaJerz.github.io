Jakub (Kuba) Jerzmanowski

Computer Science Graduate Student & Research Assistant

Hi, I’m Kuba (Jakub) Jerzmanowski a Computer Science Graduate Student at the University of South Carolina. I am interested in applying data science, especially machine learning, to address important real-world problems and improve social and economic conditions.

I am fortunate to be a part of the Valafar Group lab, where I focus on applying machine learning to time series data from medical and neuroscience applications. As part of our ongoing experiments, we conduct data collection, labeling, and model design. My research specifically focuses on human activity recognition, where we design and run experiments from labeling, training, and evaluating our models that perform gesture-level classification and segmentation, both on edge devices and in batch processing, for medical applications.
