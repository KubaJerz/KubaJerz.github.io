Jakub (Kuba) Jerzmanowski

Computer Science Graduate Student & Research Assistant

I am a Computer Science major with an Economics minor at the University of South Carolina. I will be completing my BS in Computer Science in the spring of 2025, and I will continue my studies in the Master's program at USC. I am interested in applying data analytics, especially deep machine learning, to address important real-world problems and improve social and economic conditions.

I am fortunate to be a part of the Valafar Group lab, where I focus on applying machine learning to time series data from medical and neuroscience applications. In my most recent research, we used deep ML for the recognition of micro gestures involved in taking medication. I am also involved in research on sleep-stage recognition from EEG signals in rats.

I enjoy learning, tackling challenges, working in teams, and sharing my knowledge with others. I recently started to get involved in teaching by serving as a teaching assistant at the 2024 SCENIBRI Machine Learning in Bioinformatics workshop and look forward to continuing my involvement in educational initiatives.
