Jakub Jerzmanowski — Personal Assistant Texts

Jakub Jerzmanowski runs a personal assistant that answers his own text messages. He is the only recipient. The program sends no marketing and sends no messages to anyone else.

How to join

Jakub texts the keyword START to the program number from his own mobile phone. He gets one confirmation text. No other phone number can join, and no phone number is collected from anyone else.

Terms

  • Program: Jakub Jerzmanowski Personal Assistant Texts.
  • Messages: replies to Jakub's own questions, and reminders he asked for, about his calendar, tasks, and email.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP to opt out at any time. You get one confirmation and no more messages.
  • Text HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy policy

  • The program stores the messages it sends and receives, only to give replies.
  • Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • No data is sold.
  • Questions: text HELP to the program number.
